# ball8n

**Price A/B testing and profit analytics for Amazon sellers.**

ball8n helps Amazon sellers find the price that actually maximises profit. It doesn't rely on gut feel or competitor-chasing. Sellers run controlled price experiments on their own listings and see the effect on sales, units and contribution margin.

### What it does

- **Price experiments.** Rotate a listing between price points on a schedule and compare the arms side by side to pick a winner.
- **Margins you can trust.** Margins are built from Amazon settlement data and your own unit costs, so fees, refunds and COGS are already deducted.
- **Sales and traffic analytics.** Orders, sessions and conversion are synced daily from the Selling Partner API.
- **Product groups.** Test and report on variations and bundles together.
- **Works with Claude.** A built-in MCP server lets you ask about your experiments and margins straight from Claude.
